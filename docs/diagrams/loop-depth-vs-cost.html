Where the money stops buying review

Review depth is already at its ceiling by ultra — nine lanes, three reviewers each, three rounds. The last step up the ladder adds no further reviewers at all. It buys the same eighty-one invocations on a more expensive model, and that is the entire difference between 3× and 8×.

Depth = lanes × reviewers per lane × rounds, the maximum reviewer invocations a run can make: 1 · 8 · 27 · 81 · 81. Cost = the multiplier in the loop table: 0.1× · 0.3× · 1× · 3× · 8×. Both series are indexed to their own maximum so two different units share one axis; the absolute figures are printed above. Rounds 2 and 3 rerun only the lanes that owned accepted findings, so depth is a ceiling, not a typical run.
