Escalation is one way

You pick a loop once, but the run can overrule you. Every trigger climbs; nothing descends. A Critical never gets fixed in place and carried on with — the concerns the lighter loop compressed have not had proper attention, and a change that produced one was misjudged.

Transitions and their guards are quoted from the escalation table in CLAUDE.md. Reviewer counts are lanes × reviewers per lane × rounds. Escalating from dev-loop upward is rarer than escalating into it, and is stated to the user with the cost rather than taken silently.
