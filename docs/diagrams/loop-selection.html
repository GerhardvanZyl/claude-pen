Match the loop to the blast radius

The frame is centred on the full loop, so every other option reads as a displacement from the default rather than a point on an abstract scale. Reaching past the point where the extra scrutiny changes the outcome is spending, not diligence — and so is the opposite.

Horizontal position is the cost multiplier on a log scale — 0.1× · 0.3× · 1× · 3× · 8×. Vertical position is a judgment, not a measurement: it reads the selection criteria in CLAUDE.md, from “easy to roll back” at the bottom to “severe and largely irreversible” at the top. The dashed line is the claim the ladder makes about itself — that each step up buys scrutiny in proportion to what a miss would cost.
