The shape of each loop

Five dimensions of review depth, each normalised to its own maximum. The full loop is not a smaller version of the heaviest one — it is a different shape. It takes breadth and repetition to the ceiling and deliberately leaves reviewers-per-lane and model tier on the floor.

All five axes normalised to 0–10 against the maximum in the loop table. Lanes: 1 · 4 · 9 · 9 · 9. Reviewers per lane: 1 · 1 · 1 · 3 · 3. Rounds: 1 · 2 · 3 · 3 · 3. Opus share of reviewer slots: 0% · 0% · 44% · 44% · 100%. Verification: never = 0, conditional = 5, always = 10. Two axes read zero for ultralight, which is why its polygon is pinned to the centre.
